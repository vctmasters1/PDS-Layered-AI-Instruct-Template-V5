# Analysis: Electrical Engineer (Transmission & Distribution) at Morson Talent (Canada & USA)

## Key Requirements
- Minimum 7 years of Transmission & Distribution (T&D) substation engineering experience for electric utilities
- Specific project experience with PG&E and renewable energy power plants
- Electrical Project Engineer background in substation applications
- Primary (outdoor) physical substation design and engineering studies
- Hands-on production of power single line diagrams, substation plan/elevation drawings, general arrangements, equipment erection drawings, and construction details
- Layout expertise for high voltage bus, cable, HV equipment, conduit, wiring, auxiliary power, grounding, and control buildings
- In-depth application of North American HV substation standards: IEEE, ANSI, NEC, NESC, and local Grid Codes
- Experience with both Air-Insulated Switchgear (AIS) and Gas-Insulated Switchgear (GIS)
- Bachelor’s Degree in Engineering (BSEE preferred)
- Active US Professional Engineering (PE) license or documented plan to obtain
- Willingness to review, approve, and seal engineering work
- Remote work capability (California-based or authorized for US contract)
