## Resume Style Guide

**Structure & Ordering**
*   **Header:** Name on the first line. Second line: `City, State | Phone | Email | URL`.
*   **Section Order:** Professional Summary → Work Experience → Skills → Technologies → Education and Training.
*   **Headers:** Use clear, capitalized section titles.

**Professional Summary**
*   Write 3–4 sentences.
*   Open with role/title and years of experience.
*   Highlight 1–2 major architectural or operational achievements with quantifiable impact.
*   Conclude with core competencies or value propositions.
*   Tone: Authoritative, results-oriented, and concise.

**Work Experience**
*   **Entry Format:**
    `Company Name | City, State`
    `Job Title | Month Year – Month Year`
*   **Date Format:** `Month Year – Month Year` or `Month Year – Present`. Use en-dashes.
*   **Bullet Points:**
    *   Start every bullet with a strong action verb (e.g., *Drive, Engineer, Lead, Build, Design*). Never use "Responsible for."
    *   **Quantification:** Mandatory. Include metrics, scales, percentages, or financial impact (e.g., "4× increase," "100% safety record," "[X]+ wells").
    *   **Focus:** Emphasize outcomes and business value over routine duties. Connect technical actions to operational improvements.
    *   **Length:** 1–2 lines per bullet. Concise but dense with information.
    *   **Placeholders:** If specific numbers are unknown, use brackets like `[X]` to indicate where quantification is needed, but prefer concrete data.

**Skills & Technologies**
*   **Skills:** Comma-separated list of domains, tools, and methodologies (e.g., SCADA Systems, PLC Programming).
*   **Technologies:** Separate section for programming languages, frameworks, and OS (e.g., Python, SQL, Linux).
*   **Format:** Single paragraph, comma-separated. No bullet points within these sections.

**Education and Training**
*   **Format:** `Degree/Coursework Details | Institution Name`.
*   Use pipe separator `|` between details and school.

**Tone & Writing Style**
*   **Voice:** Professional, punchy, and direct. Use implied first-person (omit "I" or "my").
*   **Style:** Impact-driven. Every bullet must answer "So what?" by showing efficiency gains, cost savings, safety improvements, or scale.
*   **Vocabulary:** Use industry-specific terminology accurately. Avoid filler words.

**Layout Preferences**
*   Use pipe characters `|` for inline separators in the header and education section.
*   Maintain consistent spacing between sections.
*   Ensure hierarchy is clear: Company/Title distinct from bullets.
