# Analysis: Controls Manager (Industrial Automation Engineer) at Amazon Data Services, Inc.

## Key Requirements
- B.S. in Electrical/Mechanical/related engineering (or Associate + 12 yrs, or HS + 15 yrs controls experience)
- 10+ years of controls-related experience
- 6+ years hiring, promoting, coaching, leading, and developing controls teams
- 6+ years with industrial controls in critical environments (data center, pharma, manufacturing, oil & gas, etc.)
- 6+ years general project or vendor management (RFPs, bidding, change orders, QC, RFI/submittal tracking)
- Installation, programming, and troubleshooting of BMS/EPMS and/or industrial controls systems
- Strong technical writing and communication skills
- Proficiency reading/interpreting electrical diagrams, control diagrams, control circuits, and industrial HVAC systems
- Ability to develop project scope, specifications, and align engineering work with design intent
- Experience developing control panel, power distribution, and junction box drawings (GA, schematics, wire diagrams)
- Experience developing logic diagrams in IEC 61131-3 format for PLCs/DCS
- Experience performing inspections, testing, and commissioning of facility systems
- Cross-functional project coordination to ensure on-time, on-budget delivery
- Willingness to travel up to 30% domestically
- Preferred: M.S. in Engineering, PMP, PE/LEED certification, AWS/Amazon experience, Military service
