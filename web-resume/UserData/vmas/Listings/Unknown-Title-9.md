About the job

The Vision

Generative AI is stepping out of the browser and into the physical world. At GlobalLogic/Hitachi, we are building "Physical AI"—bridging digital agentic intelligence with real-world mechanical and industrial systems. While our prototyping engineers build the physical lab foundations, we need visionary architects to scale these innovations into actual enterprise environments. We target massive operational impact across Hitachi’s global industrial footprint and our Fortune 500 customers. We are leading the Physical AI revolution, transforming how asset-heavy industries operate.




The Role

You sit at the intersection of Physical AI capabilities, IT/OT systems integration, and customer enterprise transformation. While similar to our Physical AI engineers, this is a customer-facing solutioning role rather than an active hands-on prototyping position. You will act as the critical bridge between our advanced AI labs and our industrial clients.




Your job is to translate cutting-edge agentic AI models and physical prototypes into scalable, secure, and highly integrated architectures for real-world manufacturing and industrial facilities. You are highly technical, but your true passion is seeing the tangible impact of your architectures through successful customer transformation.




What You Will Do

Customer Solutioning & Architecture: Architect comprehensive Physical AI solutions that seamlessly integrate advanced AI models with existing IT systems and Operations Technology (OT) on the manufacturing floor.
Bridge the Digital and the Physical: Translate the capabilities of autonomous AI agents into concrete, scalable industrial workflows. You will take the innovations developed by our hardware hackers and map them to standard industrial automation realities.
Drive Customer Transformation: Lead technical workshops and deep-dive discussions with client engineering and operational leaders. You will communicate complex AI concepts into actionable operational strategies, making clients feel confident in adopting the Physical AI revolution.
IT/OT Integration Strategy: Design secure, robust data pipelines bridging modern AI software environments with standard industrial protocols, enterprise systems (e.g., AVEVA PI, SAP, Maximo), and PLCs.
Impact Realization: Continually align technical solutioning with measurable impact on the actual manufacturing facilities, focusing on execution velocity, safety, and operational ROI.




What We Are Looking For

(Note: The CTO AI office is a strictly AI-first environment - we provide, and expect you to heavily leverage, a Gemini Ultra subscription to accelerate your architectural design, research, and daily workflows so you can focus purely on high-level solutioning and customer success.)

OT & IT Integration Expert: Deep expertise in connecting modern cloud/IT infrastructure with OT systems. You understand the realities of legacy industrial environments.
Manufacturing Acumen: A strong understanding of manufacturing processes, supply chain logistics, or energy grids. You must be able to credibly discuss your past impact on actual manufacturing facilities.
The Transformation Communicator: You possess high emotional intelligence and excellent communication skills. You know how to sit down with senior operational leaders, translate AI hype into structural reality, and guide them through a massive technological shift.
Highly Technical Foundation: While you won't be soldering microcontrollers daily, you must be deeply technical. You understand APIs, AI agentic frameworks, and system architecture.
Industrial Automation (Bonus): Hands-on past experience with or strong understanding of manufacturing automation (e.g., standard PLCs like Siemens, Allen Bradley, or Mitsubishi) is a significant plus.




Why Join Us?

This is the ultimate opportunity to apply advanced AI to the physical world with massive real-world stakes 8. You will take cutting-edge AI models out of the sandbox and deploy them at scale, transforming global industries and defining the future of physical operations.




Experience & Education Requirements

Experience: 7+ years dedicated to technical solution architecture, IT/OT integration, or systems engineering, with a proven track record in customer-facing technical leadership.




Domain Expertise: Proven experience working with asset-heavy industries (Manufacturing, Energy, Utilities) and deep familiarity with their IT/OT environments. Must be able to demonstrate past impact on physical manufacturing facilities




Education: Bachelor’s or Master’s in Computer Science, Systems Engineering, Industrial Engineering, or a related field.
