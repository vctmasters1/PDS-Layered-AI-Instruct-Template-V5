The Senior Solution Architect for Mission Critical Controls/ Data Center is responsible for designing, integrating, and optimizing mission-critical systems that support real-time BMS/EPMS monitoring, control, and analytics. This role bridges business needs, operational requirements, and technical architecture to ensure high availability, security, scalability, and performance of data center mission critical control environments.

Minimum Requirements

Education: Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Engineering, Information Systems, or related field

Experience: Experience with monitoring, control systems, or real-time data platforms. Proven experience as a Solution Architect, Systems Architect, or similar role. Knowledge of mission-critical environments using PLC’s, SCADA, Rockwell and Ignition software, EPMS software knowledge. preferred. Architecture or cloud certifications a plus.

Travel: 30-50%

Work Schedule: Typical work hours are between 7:00 a.m. and 5:00 p.m. Monday – Friday. However, work may be performed at any time on any day of the week to meet business needs.

Key Responsibilities


Designs end-to-end solution architectures for mission control systems, including data gathering, processing, visualization, and BMS/EPMS control platforms
Defines architecture standards, reference designs, and best practices for mission-critical data center solutions
Ensures solutions meet requirements for availability, resilience, latency, and scalability
Integrates infrastructure, applications, OT systems, and monitoring tools into a unified mission control environment
Collaborates with client, customer, consulting engineers and vendor to ensure seamless interoperability
Oversees integration of vendors platforms and provide guidance in developing FTI mission critical solutions
Works closely with business leaders, operations teams, and engineers to translate operational needs into technical solutions
Leads design reviews, and architecture discussions to create a client functional design specification
Provides technical guidance throughout the project lifecycle
Embed monitoring, alerting, and incident response capabilities into solutions
Supports implementation teams during deployment, testing, and go-live phases
Validates that delivered solutions align with approved architecture designs
Troubleshoots complex system and integration issues
Evaluates emerging technologies relevant to mission control and data center operations
Recommends improvements to performance, automation, observability, and cost efficiency
Contributes to long-term technology roadmaps
Strong understanding of data center infrastructure, networking, and distributed systems
Ability to communicate complex technical concepts to non-technical stakeholders
Systems integration across both BMS, EPMS and knowledge of electrical and mechanical systems
High-availability and resilience design
Analytical thinking and problem solving
Strong documentation and communication skills
Reliable, scalable mission control solutions that meet operational demands
Strong alignment between business goals and technical architecture
Work strongly with the FTI teamwork and culture
Performs other related duties as required and assigned.
