Purpose of Role

We're seeking a Strategic Technology Architect to define and guide the next-generation architecture for Tait's mission‑critical ecosystem. This role is ideal for a visionary systems thinker who explores emerging technologies, evaluates their commercial and operational value, and can articulate a clear architectural path forward.

You will be one of the key architectural voices within the CTO Office - shaping long‑term architecture, conducting deep independent research, and identifying how emerging capabilities across LMR, LTE/5G/6G, AI/ML, sensing, cloud/edge, and automation can materially improve outcomes for public safety, utilities, and transportation customers.

This is not a theoretical architecture role, nor a hands‑on POC-builder role. You will connect technology foresight with customer value, business impact, and strategic investment - ensuring Tait's architecture evolves with clarity and purpose.

Public safety, utilities, and transportation are undergoing a once‑in‑a‑generation technological shift. AI, cloud, sensing, and 5G→6G will redefine what "mission‑critical" means.

By Joining Tait, You Will


Shape the future architecture of mission‑critical communications and field intelligence
Influence technology that thousands of field workers and first responders rely on daily
Drive innovation that improves safety, resilience, and operational effectiveness
Explore emerging technologies and determine which ones become real products


If you want to combine architecture, exploration, and meaningful real‑world impact - this role gives you a rare opportunity to do exactly that.

Key Responsibilities and Outcomes


Define the next‑generation mission‑critical architecture across LMR → LTE/5G → 6G, cloud/edge, AI‑enabled capabilities, and multi‑modal sensing.
Continuously investigate emerging technologies and evaluate their strategic fit for mission‑critical environments.
Analyze trends, technology readiness, ecosystem maturity, and adoption scenarios.
Provide clear architectural recommendations rooted in technical depth and real operational constraints.
Assess market signals, competitive movement, and adoption trajectories to guide timing and investment.
Partner with product and commercial teams to ensure architectural directions are backed by strong business cases, customer value drivers, and go‑to‑market potential.
Communicate emerging technology and architectural concepts in terms of customer benefit, ROI, risk reduction, and strategic impact - not just technical feasibility.
Guide internal innovation teams by defining architectural hypotheses and evaluation criteria for POCs (executed by other teams).
Work closely with engineering, product, and standards groups to drive alignment with the long‑term architecture.
Support the CTO with architectural insights, strategic assessments, and technology direction proposals.
