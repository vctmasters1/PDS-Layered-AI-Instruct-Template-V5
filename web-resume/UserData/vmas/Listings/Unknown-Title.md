About the job

Company Description

Inpixon® is a global leader in Indoor Intelligence®, specialized in helping industrial operations, logistics, and government teams optimize performance and safety. By leveraging a technology-agnostic RTLS platform that integrates UWB, BLE, GPS, and more, Inpixon turns location and operational data into actionable insights and automated responses. Trusted by customers worldwide, Inpixon’s cutting-edge solutions improve workflow efficiency, enhance site safety, and reduce operational costs. With its innovative conversational AI, askPixi, the company bridges the gap between real-time location systems and enterprise platforms like ERP, MES, and WMS, enabling faster decision-making and risk mitigation.




Role Description

You’ll harness innovative technology to deliver business value—partnering with customers to design and deliver Real-Time Location System (RTLS) solutions that transform how factories, warehouses, and industrial facilities operate. You’ll work across the full solution lifecycle: from early-stage technical discovery and solution architecture, through hands-on deployment and integration, to training and long-term customer success.




During your first year, expect to be highly hands-on—getting into customer facilities, installing hardware, deploying software, and building the deep product expertise that makes you an invaluable technical advisor.




What You’ll Do

Pre-Sales & Solution Design

Partner with Sales to lead technical discovery sessions and translate business needs into tailored RTLS architectures
Design solutions using Inpixon’s product suite to address manufacturing, logistics, and warehouse challenges
Deliver compelling technical presentations, live demos, and proof-of-concept engagements
Contribute to RFP/RFQ responses with clear, persuasive technical proposals

Deployment & Professional Services

Conduct on-site planning and RF surveys at customer facilities
Install and configure Inpixon sensor hardware (anchors, gateways, routers) in industrial environments
Deploy the Intranav software platform on-premises within customer IT/OT environments
Coordinate with customer IT, OT, and security teams for secure, compliant system integration
Troubleshoot and resolve deployment and integration issues during pilots and full-scale rollouts

Product Expertise & Feedback

Develop deep technical mastery of Inpixon’s RTLS products, architecture, and deployment models
Identify product gaps and opportunities from the field and communicate actionable feedback to Product and Engineering

Training & Customer Success

Train customer teams on effective platform use and recommend process improvements to maximize ROI
Create technical documentation, training materials, and knowledge-transfer sessions
Act as a trusted technical advisor throughout the customer lifecycle

Industry Engagement

Represent Inpixon at tradeshows, conferences, and customer events
Present our solutions to both technical and business audiences




What We’re Looking For
