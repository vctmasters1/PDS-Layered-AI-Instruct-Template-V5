The Solutions Integration Architect oversees the architecture, design, and testing of the integrations & interoperability of software programs, vendor applications, and third-party solutions; these duties would include data integration and workflow design of integration solutions in consultation with analysts, designers, and system owners of vendor application systems. As the subject matter expert, the solutions integration architect must understand client requirements and how Landis+Gyr solutions fit into these requirements. The ideal candidate will be technical-focused, possess strong bsuiness aptitude and excellent client-facing communication skills, and understand electric utility power system operations and related IT subsystems. Successful candidates will be able to bridge the gap between business and technology, manage client resources, drive technical solutions to timely completion, and identify, analyze, and extract key data from heterogeneous systems. This role works closely with a wide variety of customers from senior project managers to developers, business units within L+G and the customer’s team to generate solutions and helps the utility customer understand the solution and its impact on utility operations.

Key Responsibilities and Accountabilities:


 Consult with customers to scope, design, document, implement and deliver system integration solutions to accomplish their business goals.
 Architect new solutions and deliver new services, which meet the needs of the customer.
 Work directly with customers to create data mappings involved in architecting the integration solution.
 Actively participate in the evolution of integration best practices, tools and processes within Landis+Gyr.
 Collaborate with analysts, designers, and system owners in the testing of newly-integrated software programs and applications.
 May install and configure software at customer's site and perform user acceptance testing and mentoring of customer staff while onsite.
 Understand data quality and how data quality can impact the final integrated solution.
 Support integration testing phase of software and applications under development in order to identify and remedy potential problem areas.
 Plan for, test and troubleshoot integration interfaces.
 Ensure that integration projects meet business requirements and goals, fulfill end-user requirements, and identify and resolve systems issues.
 Provides integration interface enhancement ideas to Product Management to drive future improvements around ease of integration and service delivery.
 Develop standards, best practices, patterns, and processes to support and facilitate integration projects.
